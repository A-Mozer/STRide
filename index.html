Learn More

Forensically validated STR markers for equine
identification and wildlife crime investigation

The STRide project develops a robust tetranucleotide STR multiplex with improved accuracy,
reproducibility, and interpretability. By addressing key limitations of current equine genetic markers, it
provides a reliable tool for forensic applications. The resulting system will support investigations of
wildlife crime, animal cruelty, pedigree fraud, and human cases involving horse and wild equid evidence.

Applications

Forensic and conservation applications of the STRide system:

  • Animal Cruelty

    Supports investigations of animal cruelty cases, including severe abuse incidents such as “horse-ripping” events.

  • Human Crime Investigations

    Assists criminal investigations where equine evidence is present, such as horse hair on suspects or traces from crime scenes involving stables.

  • Pedigree Testing

    Provides reliable genetic verification for pedigree authenticity in horse breeding and trade.

  • Theft

    Enables forensic identification in cases of horse theft and the theft of high-value equestrian equipment.

  • Illegal Wildlife Trade

    Supports the detection of illegal trade involving wild equids and related products and other derivatives.

  • Genetic Diversity

    Enables assessment of genetic diversity in wild equid populations, contributing to conservation strategies and protection measures.
